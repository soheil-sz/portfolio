At my last internship, I observed a PCB fabrication lab, took part in solder mask application and supervised the Pick and Place machine and the reflow ovens.
Next, I transformed a 3D printer gantry into an Automatic Optical Inspection machine for the PCBs. I used a Raspberry Pi to automate the S-curve movement and synchronized it with the camera. Precise control of the machine let us generate high quality datasets for the ML team.

I wanted to turn a vintage transistor radio into a Bluetooth speaker, while conserving its outer appearance. To do that, I designed my first PCB in EasyEDA, based around the TDA7379. I then ordered the board and soldered on the components and the auxiliary circuits (USB charging and Bluetooth module).
