At my last internship, I worked in a PCB fabrication lab, took part in solder mask application and supervised the Pick and Place machine and the reflow ovens.

Next, I transformed a 3D printer gantry into an Automatic Optical Inspection machine for the PCBs. I used a Raspberry Pi to automate the S-curve movement and synchronized the gantry with the camera. Precise control of the machine let us generate high quality datasets for the ML team, and we reduced inspection times from 10 min/board to approx. 30 sec/board.
